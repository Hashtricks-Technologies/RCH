# RCH

Inventory Management System with integrated Billing.

## Status

Project initialized — scope and architecture still being ideated.

## Scope (working draft)

An inventory management system where stock movement and billing are a single
flow, not two disconnected modules. Issuing a bill draws down stock; receiving
stock updates valuation and availability immediately.

### Expected core areas

- **Catalog** — items, categories, units of measure, batch/expiry, barcodes
- **Stock** — inbound (GRN/purchase), outbound (issue/sale), transfers, adjustments, stock-take
- **Suppliers & purchasing** — purchase orders, receipts, supplier ledger
- **Billing** — invoices, taxes, discounts, payment modes, returns/credit notes
- **Reporting** — stock on hand, valuation, fast/slow movers, sales & tax summaries
- **Access control** — roles for admin, store, billing counter
